About

From Weddings to Wilderness

Ron Delhaye is a Colorado-based photographer who traded ballrooms for backcountry. After years behind the lens at weddings, he followed the light into the high country — and stayed.

His work is about patience: returning to the same grove, the same valley, until the land shows you something. What you see here is what was there — golden hour, held still.
