About

Patience in the High Country

Ron Delhaye is an Aspen-based fine art photographer whose work is governed by stillness, altitude, and the discipline of waiting. After more than a decade photographing private commissions across North America, he turned his lens permanently toward the backcountry of the Elk Mountains and the Roaring Fork Valley.

His work is about patience: returning to the same grove, the same valley, until the land shows you something. What you see here is what was there — golden hour, held still.
